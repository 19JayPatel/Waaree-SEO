About Waaree Energies

Pioneering India's renewable energy revolution with innovation, integrity, and impact for over two decades.

Our Journey

Founded in 1989, Waaree Energies Limited began with a vision to make renewable energy accessible and affordable to the masses. Over 25 years, we've transformed from a small startup into India's leading solar energy manufacturer and solutions provider.

Our journey has been marked by consistent innovation, strategic expansion, and an unwavering commitment to excellence. Today, with manufacturing facilities across India and a presence in over 50 countries, Waaree continues to lead the renewable energy revolution.

We believe that solar energy is not just an alternative—it's the future. Our mission is to make this future accessible to everyone.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be the world's leading provider of clean, affordable, and sustainable energy solutions.

We envision a world where solar energy powers homes, businesses, and industries, creating a sustainable future free from the constraints of fossil fuels and environmental degradation.

Our Mission

To accelerate the global transition to renewable energy through innovative solar solutions and sustainable practices.

We're committed to delivering high-quality, cost-effective solar products and services that empower individuals and organizations to embrace clean energy while contributing to a greener planet.
