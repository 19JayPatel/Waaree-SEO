Rajasthan Solar Park - 250 MW

Location: Rajasthan, India

Capacity: 250 MW

Type: Utility-Scale Ground-Mounted

Status: Operational

One of India's largest solar installations, this 250 MW solar park generates clean electricity for the state grid. The project features advanced tracking systems and real-time monitoring, delivering consistent power generation and contributing significantly to India's renewable energy targets.

Grid-Connected Ground-Mounted 95% Uptime

Premium Business Complex - 5 MW

Location: Bangalore, Karnataka

Capacity: 5 MW

Type: Rooftop Solar + Net Metering

Status: Operational

This large commercial complex achieved 70% energy independence with our integrated rooftop solar solution. The system features advanced battery storage, enabling uninterrupted operations during grid outages and reducing energy costs by approximately ₹1.2 crore annually.

Net Metering Battery Backup 70% Energy

Smart Housing Community - 15 MW

Location: Pune, Maharashtra

Capacity: 15 MW (1000+ homes)

Type: Residential Community Solar

Status: Operational

A landmark residential community project installing solar systems on 1000+ individual homes. Each household achieved complete energy independence with average monthly electricity bill reduction of 90%. The community also features a shared battery storage facility for collective benefits.
