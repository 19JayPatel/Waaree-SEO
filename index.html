About Waaree Energies

Waaree Energies Limited is India's leading integrated solar company, specializing in the design, manufacturing, and distribution of high-quality solar modules, inverters, batteries, and complete solar energy solutions.

With state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities and a team of dedicated professionals, we've been instrumental in accelerating India's transition to renewable energy. Our commitment to innovation and sustainability has earned us recognition as a trusted partner for residential, commercial, and utility-scale projects across the globe.
