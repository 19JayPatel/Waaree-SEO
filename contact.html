Frequently Asked Questions

What is the typical ROI for a residential solar system?

Most residential solar systems achieve ROI within 5-7 years through electricity bill savings. Government subsidies and tax incentives further improve returns.

How long do solar panels last?

Our solar panels come with 25-year warranties and typically last 30+ years. They maintain 80% efficiency after 25 years.

Do I need battery storage?

While not mandatory, battery storage provides backup power during outages and maximizes energy utilization. It depends on your specific needs and grid availability.

What about maintenance?

Solar systems require minimal maintenance—mainly annual cleaning and occasional inspections. We provide comprehensive maintenance packages.

Is financing available?

Yes, we offer flexible financing options through partner banks and NBFC institutions with attractive interest rates and long tenure.

Can I monitor my system?

Yes! Our smart monitoring systems allow real-time tracking of energy generation, consumption, and system health via mobile app.
